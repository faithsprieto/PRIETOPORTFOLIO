1. Capstone Project — Mentorch

A school-based peer mentoring platform designed to facilitate academic assistance, collaboration, and resource sharing among students within the same institution. The platform connects students with peers who can provide guidance in specific subjects or areas of expertise, while providing a centralized space for students to store and share academic resources.

The project was developed to address gaps in existing Learning Management Systems (LMS) by introducing features focused on peer-to-peer mentorship, student collaboration, academic resource sharing, and community building within departments and across the institution.
